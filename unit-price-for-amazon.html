Privacy Policy

Unit Price for Amazon · Chrome Extension · Last updated May 2026

This extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything runs locally on your device.

What the extension does

Unit Price for Amazon reads Amazon search result pages that you already have open in your browser. It calculates and displays a unit price (per kg, liter, count, etc.) on each product card, and shows a sortable comparison list in Chrome's side panel. All of this happens entirely on your device — no data ever leaves your browser.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your browsing history or search queries
  • Which products you view or click
  • Your location or device information
  • Any usage analytics or crash reports

Local storage (on your device only)

The extension saves two things only on your own device, never on any server:

  • Your preferences — theme (light/dark), view density, sort order — so they persist between sessions
  • A short-lived search cache — the last set of products seen, kept for 30 minutes so the panel loads instantly when you reopen it

You can clear this data at any time: chrome://extensions → Unit Price → Details → Clear storage.

Network requests

This extension makes zero network requests of its own. It reads only pages that your browser has already loaded from Amazon. No information is sent to any server, including ours.

Third parties

No data is shared with any third party. The extension has no ads, no analytics (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no similar tools), and no affiliate tracking that involves transmitting your data.

Children's privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing to apply special rules to.
